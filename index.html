    How to play: choose 1 in the menu, type two different names, then drop pieces with the number buttons or your keyboard (1–7). After a game, choose 2 to see the ranking: a win gives 3 points, a draw 1 point each. Try typing abc or 9 to see the input checks.

    How it works: the four folders were compiled together with clang to WebAssembly. The page only supplies printf, scanf and getchar; every rule, message and score comes from our C code.
